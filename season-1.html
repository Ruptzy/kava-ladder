The club

Downtown Bradenton, Florida · since 2021

The club started in 2021, just after COVID, when a handful of players began meeting at Kava Social in downtown Bradenton. When the original organizer moved on, Harold Gonzalez took over as director and built it into what it is today.

Stronger players teach the rest. Study nights work through positions as a room. FIDE Masters come in for lectures and simuls. Anyone who wants rated chess can play US Chess events here.

Harold also opened the club up to the wider game — the Manasota Chess Center, the Tampa, St. Petersburg, Orlando and University of Florida chess communities, Chess67 and US Chess organizers — and members travel together to tournaments across the state.

The league came later. For years it was just chess nights. The ladder, the brackets and the ratings grew out of the club, not the other way round.

It was never about who’s best in the room. This is a community that happens to play chess, and nobody is turned away for being new or rusty.

How the ratings work

  • Win and your number goes up. Lose and it goes down.
  • Beat someone rated higher: big gain. Lose to someone lower: big loss.
  • The ± is how settled your number is. It shrinks as you play.
  • About 8 games gets you on the main table.
  • Your bracket is set on your first night and lasts the season.
  • No games for 90 days puts you on the away list. Play once and you are back.
  • Cups: top three in your bracket on a night. Three games needed.
  • The 10+, 25+, 50+ tag by a name: club nights they have come to.
